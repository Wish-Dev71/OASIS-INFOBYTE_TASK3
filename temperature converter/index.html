How It Works.

Temperature conversion formulas used by this tool.

°C

Celsius

The Celsius scale is commonly used in everyday life.

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
°F

Fahrenheit

The Fahrenheit scale is used for weather and temperature in some countries.

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
K

Kelvin

The Kelvin scale is commonly used in scientific calculations.

K = °C + 273.15
